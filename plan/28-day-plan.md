# 28-Day Plan

Each session takes 60 minutes. Use the daily split in `README.md` unless a day specifies a timed test.

## Week 1: Fundamentals and lifecycle

| Day | Focus | Work |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Read the exam overview. Answer Questions 1 to 10 in `practice/practice-questions.md` without notes. Record the score and weak topics. |
| 2 | Testing objectives | Study Week 1 sections 1 and 2. Compare testing with debugging. Make cards for error, defect, failure, root cause, QA, verification, and validation. |
| 3 | Seven principles | Study Week 1 section 3. Write one work example for each principle. |
| 4 | Test activities | Study Week 1 section 4. Put the seven activity groups in order and name one work product from each group. |
| 5 | Roles and skills | Study Week 1 section 5. Compare the testing role with the test-management role. Review independence and the whole-team approach. |
| 6 | Lifecycle | Study Week 1 section 6. Compare sequential, iterative, incremental, Agile, and DevOps work. |
| 7 | Levels and types | Study Week 1 sections 7 and 8. Answer Questions 1 to 10 again. Review each mistake. |

## Week 2: Static testing and black-box techniques

| Day | Focus | Work |
|---:|---|---|
| 8 | Static testing | Study Week 2 sections 1 and 2. Compare static and dynamic testing in a two-column table. |
| 9 | Review process | Study Week 2 section 3. Memorize the five review activities and the main review roles. |
| 10 | Review types | Study Week 2 section 4. Compare informal review, walkthrough, technical review, and inspection. |
| 11 | Equivalence partitioning | Study Week 2 section 5. Solve all equivalence-partition exercises without looking at the answers. |
| 12 | Boundary values | Study Week 2 section 6. Practise two-value and three-value boundary-value analysis. |
| 13 | Decision tables | Study Week 2 section 7. Build a complete table for three Boolean conditions. Remove impossible rules only when the requirements support removal. |
| 14 | State transitions | Study Week 2 section 8. Draw the state model in the exercise. Answer Questions 11 to 20 and review mistakes. |

## Week 3: Remaining techniques, management, and tools

| Day | Focus | Work |
|---:|---|---|
| 15 | White-box testing | Study Week 3 section 1. Calculate statement and branch coverage for each example. |
| 16 | Experience-based testing | Study Week 3 section 2. Create one error-guessing list and one exploratory-testing charter for your work application. |
| 17 | Collaborative testing | Study Week 3 section 3. Rewrite three requirements as user stories with testable acceptance criteria. |
| 18 | Planning and estimation | Study Week 3 section 4. Calculate three-point estimates and compare the four estimation methods. |
| 19 | Risk-based testing | Study Week 3 section 5. List five product risks from your work and rank them by likelihood and impact. |
| 20 | Monitoring and defects | Study Week 3 sections 6 to 8. Draft a defect report from a bug you found at work. Remove confidential details. |
| 21 | Tools and recap | Study Week 3 section 9. Answer Questions 21 to 30. Review every mistake and update your weak-topic list. |

## Week 4: Exam practice

| Day | Focus | Work |
|---:|---|---|
| 22 | Weak topics | Review your three weakest topics. Use `notes/quick-reference.md`, then explain each topic without notes. |
| 23 | Full practice pack | Answer all 30 questions in 45 minutes. Spend 15 minutes classifying mistakes by chapter. |
| 24 | Official Sample A | Take `resources/official/ISTQB-CTFL-Sample-A-Questions-v1.7.pdf` in 60 minutes. Do not use notes or open the answer file. |
| 25 | Sample A review | Use `resources/official/ISTQB-CTFL-Sample-A-Answers-v1.7.pdf` to explain every wrong or guessed answer. Re-read the matching syllabus learning objective. |
| 26 | Official Sample B | Take `resources/official/ISTQB-CTFL-Sample-B-Questions-v1.7.pdf` in 60 minutes. Do not use notes or open the answer file. |
| 27 | Sample B review | Use the Sample B answer file to review mistakes. Rehearse formulas, principles, test techniques, and term pairs. |
| 28 | Final check | Take official Sample C in 60 minutes and keep Sample D as a fresh reserve. Book the exam when you score at least 32 out of 40 on two fresh mocks. |

## Recovery rule

Move an unfinished session to the next day. Do not combine two full sessions. Use Week 4 Day 22 as the first catch-up slot if you miss a day.
