# ISTQB CTFL Four-Week Study Pack

This pack prepares you for the ISTQB Certified Tester Foundation Level v4.0 exam. It assumes six months of work as an automation tester and gives you 28 study sessions of 60 minutes each.

This is an independent study project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by ISTQB. Official documents remain the property of their respective copyright holders.

## Target

- Complete all 28 sessions.
- Score at least 32 out of 40 on two timed mock exams.
- Explain each wrong answer with an ISTQB term or rule.
- Finish a 40-question mock within 60 minutes.

The exam contains 40 questions. You need 26 correct answers to pass. Your study target is higher so one difficult question set does not put the result at risk.

## Project structure

- `plan/`: the 28-day schedule
- `notes/`: weekly study notes and the quick reference
- `practice/`: original practice questions and answer key
- `progress/`: the completion tracker, mistake log, and daily notes
- `resources/`: downloaded official syllabus, sample exams, exam rules, and source index
- `AGENTS.md`: instructions for the ISTQB study coach

## Daily 60-minute routine

1. Study the assigned material for 35 minutes.
2. Solve questions or exercises for 15 minutes.
3. Write a five-line summary and update the tracker for 10 minutes.

Write your answers before you read an answer key. For each mistake, record:

- the topic
- your answer and the correct answer
- the rule that supports the correct answer
- the reason you chose the wrong option
